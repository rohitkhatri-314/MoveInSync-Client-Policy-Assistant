# AeroLogix Commute Policy (Version 1.0)
Document ID: AeroLogix_Commute_Policy_v1
Effective Date: February 15, 2026
Tenant: AeroLogix

## 1. Transport Eligibility and Warehouse Roster
1.1 Daily commute facilities are provisioned exclusively for Warehouse Operations staff, Dispatch Leads, and Fulfilment Specialists operating out of AeroLogix Air Cargo Logistics Parks.
1.2 Employees residing within a radial radius of 3.0 km to 35 km from the logistics hub are eligible for shared fleet transit.
1.3 Corporate office administrative staff residing within city limits are expected to utilize company metro passes and are excluded from cab rosters.

## 2. Shift Timings and Cutoff Windows
2.1 Operational shifts run on 24-hour rotating rotations: Shift A (06:00 - 15:00), Shift B (14:00 - 23:00), and Shift C (22:00 - 07:00).
2.2 The cutoff time for modifying or requesting evening and night logout cabs is strictly 17:00 (5:00 PM) on the day of travel.
2.3 Any cancellation attempted after the 17:00 cutoff window is flagged as an operational delay violation.

## 3. Night Shift Security Escort Protocol
3.1 AeroLogix enforces a universal security standard: an armed security escort is mandatory for ALL employees (regardless of gender, grade, or role) on drops scheduled after 21:00 (9:00 PM) or pickups prior to 05:00 (5:00 AM).
3.2 The security escort must be verified by biometric scan at the logistics bay prior to vehicle departure.
3.3 Vehicles operating without an authenticated security officer between 21:00 and 05:00 will be held at the security barrier and barred from exit.

## 4. Weekend and Holiday Travel
4.1 Weekend cabs (Saturday and Sunday) are STRICTLY PROHIBITED for all personnel across all departments without exception.
4.2 Management, directors, or team leads cannot authorize weekend cab bookings under any circumstance.
4.3 Employees scheduled for mandatory weekend inventory audits must use personal conveyance or ride-hailing services and submit an expense claim under Code TR-WKD-09.

## 5. Airport and Outstation Transfers
5.1 AeroLogix transport services operate strictly point-to-point between designated hub stations and residential clusters.
5.2 Airport pickups, drop-offs, railway station transit, and personal intermediate stops are expressly forbidden in roster cabs.

## 6. No-Show Penalties and Disciplinary Action
6.1 A 'No-Show' occurs when an employee is absent when the vehicle reaches the designated pickup coordinates with a 5-minute wait buffer.
6.2 In lieu of payroll deductions, AeroLogix operates a strict disciplinary suspension:
- 1st unexcused No-Show: Automatic suspension of all transport privileges for one calendar week (7 days).
- 2nd unexcused No-Show: Complete revocation of transport allocation for the remainder of the quarter.
