# AeroLogix Supply Chain – Fleet Commute & Logistics Transit Policy
Document ID: AeroLogix_Commute_Policy_v1
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: February 15, 2026
Applicability: Warehouse Operations, Fulfilment Leads & Air Cargo Staff
Tenant: AeroLogix

---

## 1. Transport Eligibility, Warehouse Rosters & Operational Scope
1.1 **Eligible Workforce Categories**: Daily fleet commute services are provisioned exclusively for active shift employees: Warehouse Operations Specialists, Fulfilment Centre Leads, Cargo Handlers, and Air Freight Dispatch Supervisors stationed at AeroLogix Multi-Modal Logistics Hubs.
1.2 **Corporate Staff Exclusion**: Administrative personnel, corporate legal, and finance staff based at regional headquarters are excluded from cab rosters and are provisioned with monthly corporate transit passes.
1.3 **Service Radius**: Eligible residential addresses must fall strictly within a radial radius between 3.0 km and 35 km from the designated logistics hub. Residences within 3.0 km are designated as Walk-to-Work zones and are not allocated roster seats.

## 2. Shift Windows, Advance Rosters & Cut-Off Schedules
2.1 **24-Hour Rotating Shift Schedule**: Operations run continuously across three dedicated rotations:
   - **Shift A (Morning)**: 06:00 to 15:00
   - **Shift B (Evening)**: 14:00 to 23:00
   - **Shift C (Night)**: 22:00 to 07:00
2.2 **Advance Roster Booking Windows**: Weekly shift rosters are finalized by Shift Supervisors every Friday at 12:00 PM for the upcoming operational week. Employees may view and confirm their allotted pickup routes via the MoveInSync mobile app up to 5 days in advance.
2.3 **Strict Cut-Off Window**:
   - Any modification, seat cancellation, or change of shift boarding point for evening (Shift B) and night (Shift C) departures must be logged before the strict cut-off deadline of 17:00 (5:00 PM) on the day of travel.
   - Any cancellation submitted after 17:00 is automatically classified as a late operational disruption and incurs administrative penalties.

## 3. Universal Night Shift Security Escort Protocol (All Genders)
3.1 **Mandatory Universal Security Standard**: In compliance with AeroLogix's high-security logistics park charter, an armed/certified security guard escort is mandatory for ALL employees (regardless of gender, age, or designation) on all drops scheduled after 21:00 (9:00 PM) or pickups scheduled prior to 05:00 (5:00 AM).
3.2 **Biometric Dispatch Clearance**: Security escorts must register their biometric identity scan alongside the driver at the logistics exit barrier before any vehicle departs the hub between 21:00 and 05:00.
3.3 **Zero Tolerance Gate Stop**: Any vehicle attempting to exit the logistics park boundary during night hours without a verified physical security guard on board will be physically intercepted at the perimeter boom barrier and barred from exit.

## 4. Weekend Travel Policy – Strict Absolute Prohibition
4.1 **Prohibition of Weekend Cab Allocations**: Corporate roster transport (shared cabs and vans) is strictly prohibited on Saturdays, Sundays, and national holidays without exception.
4.2 **No Managerial Discretion**: No Operations Manager, Dispatch Director, or Vice President possesses the authority to override this restriction or authorize corporate fleet transport on weekends.
4.3 **Weekend Audit Expense Claims**: Employees required to report for emergency inventory counts or weekend customs clearances must utilize self-conveyance, app-based commercial taxis, or auto-rickshaws, and claim travel reimbursement through the finance expense portal under Charge Code TR-WKD-09.

## 5. Hub-to-Hub Arterial Transit Network
5.1 **Hub-to-Hub Service**: AeroLogix transport operates strictly point-to-point along designated arterial routes connecting residential collection clusters with the logistics freight terminals.
5.2 **Scope & Route Boundaries**: Corporate fleet vehicles operate exclusively along fixed freight corridor schedules. Non-commute travel, outstation transit, railway terminal drops, and personal errands are not covered under the employee commute policy.

## 6. No-Show Violations & Disciplinary Suspension Ladder
6.1 **Boarding Window**: Drivers wait at residential pickup coordinates for a maximum of 4 minutes. If an employee fails to board, the driver triggers an unexcused No-Show.
6.2 **Disciplinary Suspension Policy**: Rather than payroll deductions, AeroLogix enforces a strict operational suspension policy:
   - **1st Unexcused No-Show**: Automatic suspension of transport facility privileges for 1 calendar week (7 consecutive days). The employee must commute independently at their own expense.
   - **2nd Unexcused No-Show (within a quarter)**: Immediate revocation of transport allocations for the entire remainder of the operational quarter.
