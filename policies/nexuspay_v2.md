# NexusPay Technologies – Corporate Employee Commute & Transport Policy
Document ID: NexusPay_Commute_Policy_v2
Version: 2.0
Effective Date: July 1, 2026
Applicability: All Full-Time Employees (Bengaluru & Hyderabad Technology Centres)
Tenant: NexusPay

---

## 1. Eligibility, Grade Hierarchy & Coverage Zones
1.1 **Eligible Employee Categories**: Corporate commute facilities (shared cabs, shuttle buses, and nodal transit) are provided strictly to full-time regular employees across Grades 1 through 5 (Software Engineers, Analysts, Senior Developers, Team Leads, and Module Leads).
1.2 **Excluded Categories**: Retainers, fixed-term contractors, external consultants, freelancers, and summer/winter interns are not eligible for daily corporate commute allocations. Contract personnel must coordinate with their third-party staffing agency for localized transport.
1.3 **Radial Distance Boundaries**:
   - **Self-Commute Exclusion Zone**: Employees residing within a 4.5 km radial distance from their designated base office are classified in the 'Self-Commute Zone' and are ineligible for daily cab rosters.
   - **Serviceable Commute Zone**: Employees residing between 4.5 km and 45 km from office are fully eligible for scheduled door-to-door or nodal roster services.
   - **Outstation / Beyond Coverage**: Locations beyond 45 km radial distance are outside serviceable boundaries; employees in this zone may utilize designated public transit hubs with corporate nodal shuttle connectivity.

## 2. Shift Schedules, Booking Windows & Cut-Off Timings
2.1 **Standard Operational Shifts**:
   - **Morning Login Windows**: 07:00, 09:00, and 11:00.
   - **Evening Logout Windows**: 16:00, 18:30, 20:30, and 22:30. (Revised evening schedule in Policy v2).
2.2 **Advance Booking Windows**: Employees may schedule and reserve their daily roster cabs up to 7 calendar days in advance via the MoveInSync mobile application or web portal. Weekly recurring rosters can be activated for fixed-shift employees.
2.3 **Cut-Off Timings for Booking & Rescheduling**:
   - **Morning Login Trips (07:00, 09:00, 11:00)**: Roster bookings, route modifications, or boarding point updates must be submitted no later than 18:00 (6:00 PM) on the preceding calendar day.
   - **Evening Logout Trips**: Logout bookings or rescheduling requests must be submitted no later than 15:00 (3:00 PM) on the same day of travel.
   - **Late Booking Policy**: The automated transport routing engine locks routes precisely at the cutoff window. Requests submitted past 15:00 for evening shifts or past 18:00 for morning shifts will be declined by the system without manual desk override.

## 3. Night Shift Operations & Female Employee Security Escort Protocols
3.1 **Mandatory Security Guard Deployment**: In accordance with revised 2026 corporate safety guidelines, a certified security guard escort is mandatory for all female employees on drop trips scheduled after 20:30 (8:30 PM). (Updated from 20:00 in Policy v1).
3.2 **Guard Duties & Verification**: The security guard must board the vehicle at the office transport bay, verify driver credentials, and accompany the vehicle until the female employee confirms safe entry inside her residence premises.
3.3 **Last Drop Safeguard (No Last Female Drop)**: Under no operational condition shall a female employee be dropped as the sole or last passenger in a shared vehicle after 20:30 without a certified security escort present in the cab. If routing dictates that a female employee is the last passenger, the assigned security guard will remain in the vehicle until her drop is finalized.
3.4 **Escort Shortage Contingency**: If an authorized security guard is unavailable at the transport bay, the vehicle dispatch is held. The transport desk will reassign a priority marshaled vehicle.

## 4. Ad-Hoc Commute, Weekend Shifts & Senior Director Approvals
4.1 **Weekend Cab Allocations**: Transport facilities on Saturdays, Sundays, and declared public holidays are strictly restricted to employees engaged in critical production infrastructure maintenance, live client releases, or disaster recovery drills.
4.2 **Approval Hierarchy for Weekend Travel (Policy v2 Revision)**: Effective Policy v2, all weekend cab bookings strictly require written approval from a Senior Director (escalated from direct Reporting Manager in Policy v1) submitted through the MoveInSync workflow portal at least 24 hours prior to the requested shift.
4.3 **Ad-Hoc Same-Day Travel**: Unscheduled ad-hoc cab requests during weekdays due to urgent production escalations require reporting manager sign-off via the MoveInSync mobile app.

## 5. Boarding Rules, Wait Buffers & No-Show Disciplinary Policies
5.1 **Driver Wait Buffer**: Cab drivers wait at the employee's designated pickup location for a maximum of 5 minutes past scheduled boarding time before marking a no-show.
5.2 **Definition of No-Show**: A No-Show is recorded whenever an employee schedules a cab and fails to board, or attempts to cancel after route generation has locked without an excused emergency.
5.3 **No-Show Financial Deduction Ladder (Policy v2 Revision)**:
   - **1st No-Show**: Automated warning email notification issued by the Transport Desk.
   - **2nd Unexcused No-Show (within a single calendar quarter)**: In revised Policy v2, a penalty of ₹500 (increased from ₹250 in v1) is deducted from the employee's subsequent monthly payroll.
   - **3rd and Subsequent No-Shows**: A penalty of ₹1,000 per incident is deducted, and corporate commute booking privileges are revoked for the remainder of the calendar quarter.

## 6. Airport Transfers, Outstation & Personal Travel Exclusions
6.1 **Airport Transfers**: Daily employee commute facilities operate strictly between residential clusters and designated office campuses. Airport pickup, airport drop-offs, and railway station transits are not covered under the employee daily commute policy.
6.2 **Corporate Business Travel**: Employees traveling for official business client meetings, flights, or outstation conferences must arrange travel via the Corporate Travel Desk under the Global Travel Expense Policy, not the daily commute platform.
6.3 **Personal Luggage & Guest Policy**: Commute cabs are communal vehicles. Carrying commercial cargo, pets, oversized household baggage, or unauthorized external guests/family members is strictly prohibited.

## 7. Transport Operations Desk, Helpline & Emergency SOS
7.1 **24/7 Transport Helpdesk**: For real-time cab tracking, delays exceeding 15 minutes, driver grievances, or lost-and-found items:
   - **Helpdesk Email**: helpdesk@nexuspay.com
   - **Internal Extension**: Ext 4401

7.2 **In-App SOS Emergency Protocol**: Every vehicle is equipped with telematics and a physical panic button. In an SOS event, triggering the panic button in the MoveInSync mobile app immediately alerts the 24/7 Security Operations Command Centre and dispatches a local emergency response vehicle.
