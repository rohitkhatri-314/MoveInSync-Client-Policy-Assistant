# NexusPay Commute Policy (Version 1.0)
Document ID: NexusPay_Commute_Policy_v1
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Tenant: NexusPay

## 1. Eligibility and General Coverage
1.1 Corporate transport facility is available to full-time employees in Grades 1 through 5 working from the Bengaluru and Hyderabad technology centres.
1.2 Employees residing within a radial distance between 4.5 km and 45 km from their designated office location are eligible for scheduled roster cab services.
1.3 Employees residing within 4.5 km are classified as 'Self-Commute Zone' and are not eligible for daily shared cabs or shuttles.

## 2. Shift Timings and Cutoff Schedules
2.1 Standard operational shifts are: Morning Login (07:00, 09:00, 11:00) and Evening Logout (16:00, 18:00, 20:00, 22:00).
2.2 The cutoff time for booking or modifying a morning pickup roster is 18:00 (6:00 PM) of the preceding business day.
2.3 The cutoff time for requesting or modifying an evening logout cab is 15:00 (3:00 PM) on the same day. Any requests submitted past 15:00 will be automatically declined by the automated roster system.

## 3. Night Shift and Female Employee Safety Escort
3.1 In accordance with corporate safety directives and state transport regulations, a security escort guard is mandatory for all female employees on drops scheduled after 20:00 (8:00 PM).
3.2 The security guard must accompany the cab from the office pickup point until the female employee is safely dropped inside her residential gate.
3.3 Under no circumstances shall a female employee be dropped as the last passenger in a shared cab after 20:00 without an armed/certified security escort present in the vehicle.
3.4 If the designated security escort is unavailable at the transport bay, the cab vendor cannot dispatch the cab. The transport desk will reassign a priority marshaled vehicle.

## 4. Ad-Hoc and Weekend Cab Allocations
4.1 Weekend cab allocations (Saturday and Sunday) are strictly restricted to critical production support and emergency database maintenance.
4.2 Weekend cab bookings require explicit prior approval from the employee's direct Reporting Manager at least 24 hours prior to the scheduled shift.
4.3 Ad-hoc requests for unscheduled travel during working weekdays require manager approval via the MoveInSync mobile application.

## 5. Cost Caps, Airport Transfers, and No-Show Penalties
5.1 Airport transfers and inter-city outstation travel are not covered under the employee daily commute policy. Employees requiring airport transit must book via the corporate travel portal under the business travel policy.
5.2 If an employee schedules a cab and fails to board without canceling prior to the cutoff window, it is logged as a 'No-Show'.
5.3 A grace threshold of one accidental no-show per calendar quarter is granted. Upon the 2nd unexcused no-show, a penalty of ₹250 will be deducted from the subsequent monthly payroll cycle.
